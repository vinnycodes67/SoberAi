
- Raw epoch 5 over-segments the pupil: predicted/true area median 1.0514, with false positives
  almost all in the boundary band (100379 band vs 169 far pixels).
- The 1.25 offset cuts that to median 1.0324 and band FP to 64938, at the cost of more band false
  negatives (1945 to 4486). Calibrated pupil predictions are still about 3% too large at the median.
- `large: 0.0` means no frames fell in that bin (true pupil area >= 4000 px), not that IoU was 0
  (the script divides by max(count, 1)).

## What this does not show

- No real iPhone captures were evaluated. Every test set is either another dataset's camera or synthetic.
- kaggle_test is near-infrared, not visible light.
- synth_test labels include some wrong frames, so its absolute numbers are noisy.
- One seed, one run. No seed variance was measured, so differences of a few thousandths (e.g. run7 vs
  run7_raw on teyed/kaggle) are not shown to be meaningful.
- The epoch 6 re-run after the restart means epoch 6 is not bit-identical to an uninterrupted run;
  epoch 5 (the chosen one) was saved before the interruption.
- Criterion 1 was checked on the every-4th-frame subset (338 frames) only.
