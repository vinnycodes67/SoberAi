Sober for iPhone

Support

Help with camera access, local data, device compatibility, and the app’s safety limits.

Sober never tells you it is safe to drive.

If you have used alcohol or another substance, feel impaired, or are unsure, do not drive. Use a ride or contact someone you trust. For an emergency, contact local emergency services.

Camera access

Sober uses a compatible iPhone’s front-facing TrueDepth camera during a check. Frames are processed on the iPhone and are not saved or uploaded.

To review access, open iPhone Settings, choose Privacy & Security, choose Camera, then find Sober. If compatible face tracking is unavailable, Sober shows an explicit unavailable state instead of inventing a reading.

Your baseline and results

Five accepted sober sessions build a personal baseline. A check can report changes, no clear read, or no changes. None of those results establishes sobriety, impairment, blood alcohol concentration, or driving safety.

Privacy Lock

Privacy Lock is optional and uses the iPhone’s system authentication. Sober does not receive or store Face ID or Touch ID data. Home and get-home actions remain available when private screens are locked.

Delete local data

In Sober, open Settings and choose Delete all local data. This removes onboarding, the Safety Plan, History, session summaries, and the measured baseline from the current installation. Sober has no server copy, but the action does not delete older iCloud or computer backups managed through Apple settings.
